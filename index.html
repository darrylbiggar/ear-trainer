How it works
Intervals begin with unison, 2nds, 3rds, 4th and 5th, then expand as your accuracy improves. By default, interval roots also progress from one fixed note to a small set, then a wider set, and finally a full random range. Chord difficulty can be set manually: major/minor only, add diminished/augmented, add sevenths, full range, or automatic progression. Chords are root-position only in this version.
