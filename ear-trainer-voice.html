How it works

After the notes finish, the app listens for your spoken answer and announces the result. It automatically advances. “Repeat” plays the notes again; “skip” reveals the answer; “stop” ends the session.

If the browser cannot recognise speech, the app switches to listening-only mode. You then answer mentally or aloud and, if the browser supports speech output, hear the correct answer after a short pause. This mode does not grade you.

Piano recordings: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0), using the same source as the original Ear Trainer. If recordings cannot load, the app uses a built-in synthesiser. Voice recognition may require internet access and permission to use the microphone. No voice recordings are saved by this app; browser speech services may process audio online.
