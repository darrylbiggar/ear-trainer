Chord Sheet Coach

Turn familiar chord sheets into harmonic functions, transpose them into a new key, and practise playing from numbers instead of chord names.

1. Paste a chord sheet

No upload required

Accepts chords above lyrics, or inline notation such as [C]first [Am]line. Section labels are retained. The sheet stays in your browser.

2. Choose how to practise

Suggested exercise

Read the Roman numerals and play the song in the selected target key. Then switch to chord names to check your answers. Try a different key without changing the song.

This version assumes a major-key tonic. It converts chord roots into scale degrees, but does not yet interpret modulations or secondary dominants such as V7/vi.
